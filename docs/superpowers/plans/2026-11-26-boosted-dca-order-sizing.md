# Confirmed DCA zones for boosted purchases

The DCA Cercle source uses confirmed monthly 2×, 3× and 4× zones to scale purchases for the next regular opening. Apply those existing zone definitions to the eight-stock boosted strategy that produced a 22.36% full-period annualized return with quarter-position sales. Test whether the zones improve the selection and size of purchases requested by the existing raw HGBT policy. ## Fixed variants

- **Priority** ranks BUY slots by the most recent confirmed monthly multiplier, then by the existing 252-session relative return, then by ticker. The multiplier applies to a BUY order only in a confirmed 2×, 3× or 4× zone. Give all other BUYs the same lower priority, so their mutual order remains the original ranking.
- **Size** uses an all-in purchase budget limit of 5% of current opening equity times the multiplier in confirmed 2×, 3× or 4× zones. This yields 10%, 15% or 20%. The existing limit is 10% elsewhere. The original post-cost symbol exposure cap, initial-capital requested amount, cash, issuer eligibility and whole portfolio constraints continue to limit any purchase. This changes the amount available to existing BUYs while preserving their original order.
- **Combined** applies the priority and size rules together.

Use the existing exact range/RSI engine with the Standard monthly profile. Use native monthly OHLCV for the original five stocks and monthly bars built from complete observed SPY session calendars for the other three. Bind the latest eligible completed month by its calendar month end at or before the model's decision close. The still open month and intramonth touches do not determine eligibility or fill size. The model decision is at the close and an eligible order executes at the following observed open. An earlier month's confirmed label can continue to apply until the next month is confirmed. Preserve the raw model probabilities, original BUY and quarter-SELL dates, sale ordering, dividend accounting, fractional share grid and no-leverage rule. Each run starts with $100,000 and receives no deposits.

Run all three variants across the six fixed reference windows through November 11, 2026. The baseline and higher-cost runs start in January 2018 and use 5 plus 5 or 25 plus 25 basis points per side. The four fresh runs start in January 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2022 at standard costs. Report return, drawdown, average cash, actual purchases by zone, fills above the former limit and matched SPY. The five standard-cost windows establish the number above 20% and the median CAGR. Consider a model-sale variant for further validation if full-period CAGR exceeds 20% under both costs, all five starts beat SPY, and the median improves on the quarter-sale reference.

Confirm independently the month-completion join, source zone classification, eight-stock support, preserved raw intents, altered size and fill order, and all cash and benchmark accounting. Use no external provider calls or new fits. The report includes every fixed arm and preserves the historical start-window and survivorship limits.
