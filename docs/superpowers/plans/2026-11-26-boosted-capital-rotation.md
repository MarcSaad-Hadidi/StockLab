# Selective purchases and capital rotation for the boosted portfolio

The current eight-stock quarter-sale strategy leaves less than 1% of equity in cash on average in each of the 2020, 2021 and 2022 fresh accounts. It earns 17.40%, 18.92% and 17.59% annualized from those starts. The diagnostic compares all eligible BUY signals on the model's fixed twenty-session open-to-open horizon. From 2024 through the end of the dataset, the 251 known signals with negative 63- and 252-session relative performance have a mean price return 1.00 percentage point below SPY. Earlier samples are mixed. Test whether the allocation can make better use of invested capital. [Quarter-sale reference](../../../ml/docs/boosted-staged-sales.md) · [Relative strength research](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1993.tb04702.x)

## Fixed comparison

- **Purchase filter** omits an original BUY when both its trailing 63- and 252-session close returns are strictly below SPY's matching returns. It leaves all other original signals as before.
- **Capital rotation** keeps the existing orders. It can fund a qualifying existing BUY by trimming the held stock with the weakest 252-session relative return. Check at the BUY's normal opening slot if that stock is ahead of SPY over both 63 and 252 sessions, its ordinary costed buy budget before applying cash is at least half of the existing 10% per-purchase limit, and available cash covers less than half of that budget. An opening with a selected regular sale is ineligible for a rotation. The donor must be distinct, held and negative on both relative windows, have an original raw HGBT score at or below .35, and have no original BUY or SELL intention on that decision date. Order eligible donors by the weakest 252-session relative return and then ascending ticker. Sell the smaller of one quarter of the donor's shares and the shares needed to fund the pre-sale cash gap. Apply existing costs and round down to eight decimals. Recompute the target budget using post-sale equity and spend only available cash. Each account may make one such completed sale and purchase per decision calendar month.
- **Combined** applies the purchase filter and capital rotation together. In all alternatives the original quarter-sale logic, model weights and forecasts, unfiltered BUY ranking, next-open prices, exposure caps and dividend rules continue to govern their relevant orders. Rotation losses and purchase fees reduce available equity. There is no leverage or cash interest.

Run eighteen accounts across the six staged-sale windows. Each family starts in January 2018 at both 5 and 25 basis points each for fees and slippage. It also starts in each of January 2019 through January 2022 at baseline costs. Use $100,000 from each start and data through November 11, 2026. Report annualized return, maximum drawdown, ending wealth, variation from the matched quarter-sale reference, buys, original partial sales, rotation sales and paired rotation purchases. Report median return and the number of starts above 20%. A further historical candidate must retain actual model sales, clear 20% in both full cost windows, beat SPY from all five standard-cost starts and improve the median across those starts.

Verify both relative returns and the causal opening sequence, parent signal binding, cash and dividend ledger, unchanged size limits, sell-to-buy linkage, and one rotation per month. Preserve the original audited account as the comparison and report every alternative. Use zero new fits or provider requests. The historical study uses one fixed, retrospective eight-stock sample.
