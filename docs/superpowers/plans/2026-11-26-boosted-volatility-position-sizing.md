# Volatility-aware sizing and partial risk sales for the boosted strategy

The retained five-stock HGBT strategy earns 18.625926% annualized with 24.765767% maximum drawdown. The eight-stock relative-sale strategy earns 17.147287% with 29.120812% drawdown. Their largest full-account losses reach a trough on March 23, 2020. The earlier next-open priority contrast only changed full five-stock CAGR by 0.002656 percentage point. Test a position-level response to material volatility instead. Prior studies motivate reducing exposure when realized risk is unusually high and report mixed real-time return results. [Volatility-managed portfolios](https://www.nber.org/papers/w22208) · [Real-time implementation evidence](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X2030132X)

## Fixed inputs and rules

- Keep the audited HGBT scores, purchase and full-sale signals, observed next opens, daily symbol order and cash/dividend conventions. Reuse the same five-stock and eight-stock ledgers as references. The latter uses the existing relative-strength full-sale filter. Freeze all source commitments before any replay. There are zero new fits, calibration calls or external requests.
- For each symbol and signal close `t`, use the population standard deviation of its last 20 observed close returns. Its normal level is the median of the preceding 252 overlapping volatility observations ending at `t-1`. Stress means the recent variance is at least twice the normal variance and the stock's 20-session close return is strictly below that of SPY. The benchmark comparison uses its existing adjusted price series. Use only price observations already available at the scheduled decision close. Require complete observed market dates from the first evaluation signal onward. Compute all episode transitions from the full calendar before any fresh-account slice.
- The position cap at the next observed opening is 20% of post-cost equity in normal conditions. In stress it becomes `20% × max(1/2, normal_volatility / recent_volatility)`. Apply a cap keyed to the preceding decision close. Leave the 10% individual-purchase limit, existing requested allocation, pooled cash, no-leverage rule and 8-decimal share precision in force. A lower purchase cap by itself creates no forced sales.
- A partial risk sale may be scheduled on entry to the simultaneous state of stress, negative 20-session stock return and original uncalibrated HGBT score at or below .35. The first score for a symbol seeds its episode state. One scheduled event per subsequent contiguous episode is consumed even with zero shares or insufficient overweight. An existing scheduled full model sale supersedes the partial sale at the same opening. A later normal full sale in that episode remains eligible. At execution sell the lesser of 25% of held shares and the shares required to reduce opening weight to the stress cap after transaction costs. Round the result downward to eight decimals. Holdings already under or at the cap produce no order. Risk sales use the same next-open fees and slippage, and cash enters the same account. Use the original event stream for all BUY signals and preserve alphabetical order across all executable intents at a common opening.

## Matched comparisons

- On five stocks compare purchase caps alone, the partial-risk-sale rule alone, and both together. On eight stocks apply the combined policy to the retained eight-stock relative-filter stream as a fixed portability check. No cutoff or variant choice follows inspection of returns.
- Each variant runs full 2018 through November 11, 2026 with 5 bps fee and 5 bps slippage, the same dates at 25 bps each, and fresh January 2021 capital at 5 bps each. In total the protocol budgets twelve replays. All accounts begin with USD 100,000 and no later deposits. Keep the original SPY benchmark identical for every matched window. The eight-stock data and its dividend events were separately reconciled.
- A candidate replacement requires full and fresh annualized growth strictly above 20%, above matched SPY and higher wealth than the frozen same-intent no-sale control, lower drawdown than the retained five-stock account and actual model sales. At high costs require above 20%, higher wealth than the same-cost retained reference and the no-sale control, lower drawdown than the retained reference, and sales. The archived no-sale controls use their original sizing. The combination versus purchase caps alone isolates the incremental effect of adding the trims under the new sizing rule. Treat partial trim count, contemporaneous risk exposure and paired differences as diagnostic. Preserve and report every result, including failed gates and the separate broader basis.
