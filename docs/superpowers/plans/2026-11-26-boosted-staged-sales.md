# Staged sales for the eight-stock boosted portfolio

The current 252-session allocation produces 21.0023% annualized over the complete window and 15.8270% from fresh January 2021 capital. The earlier eight-stock study found a material fresh-account difference between its full-exit and buy-only ledgers. Test whether retaining some of a position when the model issues a SELL improves net return across starts. Use the fixed existing allocation and apply the sale variations to exactly the same archived signals. The prior literature motivates checking how exit size and trend context interact. [Prior eight-stock study](../../../ml/docs/boosted-expanded-execution.md) · [Kaminski and Lo on stop-loss policies](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/bb69ca4b-0cdc-487f-831d-63b2e84fafee)

## Fixed comparisons

- **Buy-only control.** Remove the original SELLs from the 21% design so that retained exposure is measured using the same eight-stock allocation as the alternatives.
- **Quarter sale.** Sell one quarter of shares actually held at the next open after a retained original SELL. Keep at least 20 observed sessions between selected sale intentions in a stock, equal to the model's forecast horizon. The first eligible bearish intention starts the interval regardless of the eventual fill. Round shares down to eight decimal places.
- **Trend-confirmed full sale.** Keep an original complete sale when the stock's trailing 252-session return is strictly below SPY at the decision close. On every other original SELL, use the quarter rule. A full sale intention bypasses the spacing rule and sets the start of the next interval. Neither arm changes the base HGBT probabilities or adds a sell when the model never intended one.

Compute selection on the full dated intent stream before taking any window slice. Retain the base 20% or 30% post-cost buy caps, 10% cap per purchase, ranking in the existing BUY slots, common cash and dividend receivable accounting. Partial and complete sale orders keep their original relative positions in the same-opening sequence. Use actual next opens and unlevered $100,000 accounts. Run each arm from January 2018 at 5 basis points each for fees and slippage and again at 25 basis points each. Run fresh accounts at baseline costs from January of 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2022 through the same November 11, 2026 end. Compare all eighteen new accounts to the corresponding frozen eight-stock allocation and its matched SPY series.

## Evaluation

Report net CAGR, maximum drawdown, annual returns, buys, partial sales and full sales for the complete and fresh-window comparisons. Rank performance by the number of standard-cost starts clearing 20%, then by median annualized return across those starts, then by full-window return. A sell-enabled alternative advances to further testing when it executes sales, clears 20% annualized in both full cost settings, beats the corresponding SPY in the five standard-cost windows and improves January 2021 CAGR over the existing candidate. Drawdown is measured for the next risk phase.

Record the results for selling immediately versus holding the exact sold quantity to its twentieth later open, including gross dividends earned after the sale date and through the comparison exit. Apply the same exit costs on both opens. Keep unavailable endpoints out of the comparison. This is a paired diagnostic, while the full equity curves also account for subsequent purchases. Independently verify the original SELL membership, spacing, trend calculation, every fill and matched equity values. Bind the tested inputs, implementation and output files by SHA-256. This historical experiment requires zero refits and zero data-provider calls.
